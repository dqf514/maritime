1. Can the current system run the full daily workflow?

Verdict: yes for pilot / PoC / training-grade daily desk work — not yet as the single core production system of a large shipping company.

The commercial trunk (estimate → charter → voyage → laytime/claims → bunkers → invoices & payments) is wired through in the product desk and the automated test suite; deep email workflows, hard ERP integration, hard conflict rules on the vessel Gantt, situational AI and some report drill-downs remain demo-grade or need more depth.

3. Commercial end-to-end workflow (chartering view)

Sign in and open the workbench

  1. Open /login, tenant code demo, sign in with a chartering account.
  2. Land on the /home workbench and confirm the role shortcuts and widgets.

Master data check

  1. Open the vessel / port / counterparty master data pages; make sure there are usable vessels, load & discharge ports and charterers.
  2. If a counterparty's sanctions status is blocked, the charter cannot be activated (compliance gate).

Voyage estimate

  1. Go to /estimates and create an estimate: pick vessel and counterparty, fill quantity, freight/lumpsum, commissions, voyage days, bunkers and port costs.
  2. Hit Calculate to get TCE; clone scenarios for comparison and sensitivity.
  3. When happy, run To CP to generate a draft charter.

Page: /estimates

Charter approval and activation

  1. Go to /charters and open the charter created from the estimate.
  2. State flow: draft → pending_approval → active (no skipping).
  3. Once active, the system can link and generate the voyage (depending on seed and chain configuration).
  4. For COA scenarios, record liftings on the charter desk.

4. Voyage operations (operator view)

Departure and port calls

  1. Sign in with an operations account and open /operations/voyages.
  2. Move the voyage to in-progress; add load / discharge port calls (order, ETA, agents…).
  3. Enter noon reports (position, speed, ROB, next-port ETA); the system can flag ETA deviation.
  4. Register SOF events (e.g. NOR) under the port call — use the local timezone.

Fleet twin

  1. Open /twin for fleet situation awareness; run what-if simulations (demo grade).

The system also tracks EU ETS emissions and CII rating; carbon cost can be auto-allocated per voyage.

5. Laytime, claims and finance

Laytime and demurrage claims

  1. Sign in with demurrage or finance and open /finance.
  2. Create a laytime statement: link voyage / port calls, enter allowed time, turnaround, demurrage rate and time slices (including excluded time).
  3. After Calculate → Finalize, raise a demurrage claim and run negotiate / settle.

Clause library: 10 standard charter-party clause templates ship in (SHINC, SHEX, WWD, WWDSHEX…); charterer-specific clauses can be saved and are auto-applied at calculation time.

Statements print straight to PDF (with company letterhead) and batch-export to Excel.

Port disbursements, bunkers, invoices

  1. Port disbursements: draft PDA → submit → approve → FDA (record variance).
  2. Bunker orders: inquire → order → deliver, with ROB conservation checks.
  3. Freight invoice: draft → pending → issued → partial receipts until paid → supports GL posting.
  4. Review dynamic voyage P&L (estimate vs actual vs variance) and AR aging.

Analytics entry: /analytics

6. Ship management, approvals and dashboards

Ship management

  1. Sign in with a technical account and open /ship: certificates, PMS work orders, defects and crew snapshots (depth varies by license / integration).

Approval inbox

  1. Once charters / invoices go pending, process them in /workflows/inbox.

Dashboards

  1. Open /dashboards for the role wall; fullscreen and auto-refresh are supported.
  2. Fleet Twin supports what-if simulation; the market panel gives freight and bunker price references.

9. Limits & adoption recommendations

  • Good for: process training, pre-sales demos, pilot desks for small teams, parallel verification alongside existing systems.
  • Do not make it the single production core until: deep email/document closure, ERP posting, schedule visualization conflict strategy, key connectors and audit compliance meet your acceptance criteria.
  • Per-tenant databases can be registered and bound; business sessions currently still use the primary database by default. Cloud “one-click deploy” offerings are mostly template guidance.
  • Knowledge-Centre answers are retrieval-augmented FAQ, not real-time LLM generation.
